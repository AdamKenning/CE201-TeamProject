# Project Management Log

## Project management discussion

Our team has collaborated effectively throughout the project, maintaining clear communication during our weekly meetings. Each member has shown the ability to contribute to discussions, task completion, and problem-solving actively, allowing us to stay on course and even advance our project goals. We have effectively used the agile method, allowing us to adapt quickly to changes and challenges while maintaining a productive workflow. The delegation of tasks has been well-balanced, allowing each team member to leverage their strengths while supporting one another when needed.

Overall, the project has progressed well, with the majority of our milestones being met according to our timeline. While some minor delays have occurred due to unforeseen complexities and technical issues, the team has worked together to mitigate these issues and find solutions efficiently. Weekly check-ins, discussions, and meetings have helped keep everyone on track.

Our team has been holding regular Scrum meetings in the form of weekly check-ins. These meetings have been essential in keeping everyone on track, discussing progress, innovating on ideas and tackling issues early on. During each session, we review completed tasks, discuss ongoing work, and plan for upcoming objectives. These meetings have helped improve the efficiency of our team.

The main way to improve the efficiency of our Scrum meetings is time management. Notably, we were consistently the last team to leave the lab and even overran the 2-hour slot for the lab. While this allowed for nothing to be left for the following weeks' meeting, allowing for all issues to be resolved, it has made the efficiency drop and the quality of work during the latter half of the meetings.

While communication during the meetings was solid, outside of meetings was not perfect. we are able to communicate as either a group or to individual members using WhatsApp, I feel like while this is a good idea, it could have been utilised more to greatly improve efficiency.

## Burndown-Charts Discussion

While I believe that some tasks were able to be completed efficiently in some cases, the burndown charts display some issues with our efficiency in completing tasks, leaving more work during the next sprint and causing further issues.

However, I do believe these charts do not show the full picture as at times, we have simply, particularly towards the latter stages of the project, failed to update the Jira, which is reflected more negatively in the burndown charts than it initially should be.

## Other Areas

To improve our efficiency in the future, I would like our team to make greater use of Jira and use the burndown charts in meetings themselves to better visualise to the team of our current position, allowing them to better prioritize varying aspects of the project as and when it is necessary.
